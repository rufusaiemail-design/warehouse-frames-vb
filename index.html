Our Heritage

A family legacy
built on craftsmanship

Founded by Don and Teri Waheed, Warehouse Frames 'n Gallery has spent 27 years becoming Houston's most trusted source for custom framing and fine art. Their sons, Mark and Gregg Waheed, have been an integral part of the business for over 25 years — carrying the same hands-on dedication that built this institution.

We import container-load quantities directly from five countries, maintaining a permanent stock of over 20,000 frames across 34,000 square feet. No middleman. No compromise.

Factory Direct Pricing

We import directly from five countries, eliminating the middleman. Custom moulding runs 20–40% below typical retail — exceptional value without compromise.
